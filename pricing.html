Pricing FAQ

Are prices inclusive of GST?

Yes. Prices are shown as inclusive of GST where applicable unless the checkout page explicitly says GST is extra.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrade options may be offered from the dashboard. Any upgrade price shown at checkout will reflect current plan logic.

Does a matchmaker guarantee marriage?

No. Matchmaker service is best-effort assistance and suggestions are recommendations, not a guarantee of engagement or marriage.

When does matchmaker SLA start?

SLA starts after successful payment confirmation, profile readiness, and eligibility for the relevant assisted tier.

What counts as a contact?

A contact means access to contact or introduction flow for a profile, subject to privacy, plan rules, and platform controls.

What is unlimited contacts?

Unlimited means no normal monthly contact cap for eligible plan use. Abuse, spam, scraping, or unsafe conduct remains prohibited.

Are verification fees refundable?

Verification fees are non-refundable once verification has started with Parichayam or a verification partner.

Can parents use the account?

Family involvement is supported, but the prospective member's consent is required when a relative manages a profile.

Can I pause my plan?

Pause options are not guaranteed. Contact support if a genuine family emergency affects your active plan.

How do refunds work?

Refunds follow the published Refund Policy. The clearest full-refund window is within 7 days before matchmaker assignment and before any suggestion is delivered.
