Shipping / Service Delivery Policy

Parichayam is a digital matrimony platform. No physical goods are shipped.

1) Nature of Delivery

  • All plans and add-ons are digital services delivered to your Parichayam account.
  • No courier, parcel, or physical shipment is involved.

2) Membership and Add-on Activation

  • Digital activation is initiated after successful payment capture and verification checks.
  • Most activations are completed automatically; some flows may remain pending until backend confirmation/manual review.

3) Verification-linked Services

  • Services requiring document/admin review (for example identity/income verification) may take additional processing time.
  • Actual turnaround depends on submission quality, queue volume, and compliance checks.

4) Delays and Support

If digital service activation appears delayed, contact support with your registered details and payment reference via Contact Us. The team will check status and guide next steps.
