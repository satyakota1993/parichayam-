Founder Story

Parichayam was founded by Kota Venkateswara Rao with a simple belief: matrimony for Arya Vyshya families should feel respectful, transparent, and rooted in the way families actually make decisions. Large generic matrimony websites can show thousands of profiles, but they often miss the details that matter at home: gotra, kula deivam, native village, family expectations, and trusted introductions.

Parichayam brings those cultural details into a modern product. The platform is designed for families who want technology without losing family involvement, privacy without hiding important context, and matchmaker support without confusing fees.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help Arya Vyshya families across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana find suitable matches through a service that respects community customs, protects member privacy, and gives families practical tools to move from profile discovery to meaningful conversations.

What Makes Parichayam Different

Generic matrimony platforms such as large national websites are built for broad discovery. Parichayam is intentionally narrower and more personal. We focus on Arya Vyshya families, lower-cost assisted plans, dedicated matchmaker workflows, Telugu-family expectations, and clear family introduction support. The goal is not to overwhelm members with endless browsing; it is to help families evaluate suitable matches with context and dignity.

Our assisted tiers are designed around service, not just profile access. A dedicated matchmaker can review preferences, suggest profiles, capture decline reasons, and help both families move forward when mutual interest appears.

Community Focus

Parichayam supports Arya Vyshya families who care about gotra compatibility, kula deivam, native place, family reputation, education, profession, financial stability, and respectful communication. The platform is built to preserve those details while still giving younger members a modern, mobile-friendly experience.
