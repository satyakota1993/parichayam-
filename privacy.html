Privacy Policy

Parichayam is a community-focused matrimony service for Arya Vyshya families. We collect sensitive family and preference information only to deliver matchmaking, verification, payment, support, safety, and legal compliance services.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Parichayam collects, uses, stores, shares, protects, and deletes personal data when you use our website, member dashboard, payment flow, matchmaker services, verification services, support channels, or related communications. It applies to members, parents or relatives creating a profile on behalf of a prospective bride or groom, matchmakers, agents, staff users, and visitors.

For this policy, "personal data" means information about an identifiable individual. Some information provided for matrimony, such as photos, family details, gotra, kula deivam, income range, education, profession, horoscope details, and partner preferences, can be sensitive in a social context. We treat it with additional care even where a specific law does not label it as sensitive.

2. What We Collect

Depending on how you use Parichayam, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Identity and contact details: name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, age, gender, city, state, address where voluntarily provided, and account login identifiers.
  • Matrimony profile details: photos, date of birth or age, height, marital status, education, profession, employer or business details, income range, family background, parents' details, siblings, native place, community, sub-community, gotra, kula deivam, nakshatra, rashi, padam, horoscope information, lifestyle details, and partner preferences.
  • Family and consent details: information provided by parents, guardians, siblings, or relatives who create or manage a profile with the member's consent.
  • Verification records: Aadhaar verification status, income verification status, uploaded or submitted proof references, verification outcome, reviewer notes, and timestamps. We do not intentionally display full Aadhaar or financial documents to other members.
  • Payment records: membership tier, plan duration, order ID, payment ID, payment amount, coupon or discount, refund status, invoice references, Razorpay transaction metadata, revenue ledger records, and support notes related to billing.
  • Matchmaking activity: viewed profiles, interest actions, decline reasons, matchmaker notes, suggested matches, compatibility factors, family introduction status, and support interactions.
  • Device and security data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers made available by the browser, approximate location inferred from IP, login timestamps, failed login attempts, fraud-prevention logs, cookies, and analytics events.
  • Communications: emails, WhatsApp messages, calls, contact form submissions, grievance requests, refund requests, and admin notes needed to resolve your request.

3. Why We Collect It

We use personal data only for purposes connected to providing and protecting the Parichayam service:

  • To create and maintain your account and matrimony profile.
  • To show relevant matches, apply lineage-aware filters, prevent same-gotra conflicts where configured, and support family-led matchmaking.
  • To assign matchmakers, deliver assisted plans, create match suggestions, and coordinate family introductions with consent.
  • To verify identity, profile quality, payment status, and optional income or Aadhaar verification requests.
  • To process payments, refunds, invoices, ledger entries, commission calculations, and related support requests.
  • To send service messages, OTPs, payment updates, match notifications, support replies, and policy-change notices.
  • To prevent fake profiles, impersonation, harassment, spam, fraud, unauthorised scraping, payment abuse, and platform misuse.
  • To comply with applicable Indian laws, tax records, audit obligations, consumer complaints, law enforcement requests, and dispute resolution.
  • To improve the website, pricing clarity, service reliability, and customer support quality using aggregated or limited analytics.

4. Lawful Basis Under Indian Data Protection Law

Parichayam processes personal data for lawful purposes under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and other applicable Indian laws. Our primary basis is your consent: you provide clear affirmative consent when you register, submit a profile, upload documents, buy a membership, request verification, or ask us to contact another family. The DPDP notice and consent framework includes Sections 5 and 6 of the Act.

We also process limited data for certain legitimate uses recognised under Section 7 of the DPDP Act and for legal compliance, including fraud prevention, security logs, payment dispute handling, grievance redressal, tax or accounting records, and responding to lawful requests from authorities. We do not use your profile data for unrelated advertising or sell it to data brokers.

5. How We Share Data

We share personal data only as needed to deliver the service and protect the community:

  • Assigned matchmaker: assisted, premium, and elite members may have their profile, preferences, contact status, and match history reviewed by an assigned Parichayam matchmaker.
  • Potential matches: profile details are shown to other members according to your plan, privacy settings, mutual-interest status, and matchmaker workflow. Sensitive contact details are shared only where the service flow and consent permit it.
  • Mutual-interest families: when both sides express interest, Parichayam may share family contact details or an introduction brief with consent so families can speak respectfully.
  • Payment gateway: Razorpay receives transaction details required to create orders, verify payments, process refunds, and prevent payment fraud.
  • Verification partners: where you request Aadhaar, income, or other verification, we may share only the information required for that verification with approved service providers.
  • Firebase and Google Cloud: the platform uses Firebase/Google Cloud infrastructure to store, secure, and process operational data.
  • Legal and safety: we may share information when required by law, court order, law enforcement, payment dispute, consumer complaint, or to protect members from harm or fraud.
We do not sell member data. Parichayam does not sell matrimony profiles, family details, phone numbers, photos, or payment records to third-party advertisers or lead brokers.

6. Storage, Location, and Retention

Parichayam stores production data in Firebase and Google Cloud services. Our intended production storage region is Mumbai, India, where available for the relevant Firebase/Google Cloud service. Some supporting services, logs, email, analytics, or payment systems may process data in other locations as permitted by law and provider terms.

We retain profile and account data while your account remains active. After an account deletion request is completed, we delete or de-identify profile-visible data within a reasonable operational period, unless retention is required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, payment dispute, safety, or audit reasons.

Payment records, ledger records, refund records, grievance records, verification audit entries, and support records may be retained for up to three years after account deletion, or longer where Indian law, tax obligations, regulatory requirements, dispute handling, or court proceedings require it. Backup copies may remain for a limited period until overwritten under our normal backup cycle.

7. Your DPDP Rights

These rights reflect the DPDP Act rights and duties framework, including Sections 11 to 15 where applicable.

Subject to applicable law and verification of your identity, you may exercise the following rights:

  • Access: ask for a summary of personal data we process about you and how it is used.
  • Correction and updating: correct inaccurate or incomplete profile or account information.
  • Deletion or erasure: request deletion of your account and profile-visible data, subject to lawful retention needs.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent for optional processing. If the withdrawn consent is necessary to deliver matrimony services, some features may stop working or the account may need to be closed.
  • Data portability: request a practical export of key account and profile data where technically feasible.
  • Grievance redressal: contact our Grievance Officer if you believe your privacy request has not been handled properly.
  • Nomination: where applicable under the DPDP Act, you may nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

To exercise these rights, email support@parichayam.co.im from your registered email or use the Contact page. We may ask for account, phone, or payment verification before acting on requests so that one family member cannot wrongly access or delete another member's data.

8. Children and Minimum Age

Parichayam is not for children and is not intended for users under 21 years of age. We require members to be legally eligible for marriage under applicable Indian law and our Terms. If we learn that an underage person has created a profile, we may suspend or delete the profile and related data.

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies, localStorage, Firebase session data, and similar browser technologies to keep you signed in, remember service preferences, prevent abuse, support payment sessions, and analyse basic usage. Examples include Firebase Authentication session tokens, Razorpay payment session data, fraud-prevention logs, and Firebase Analytics events where enabled.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. If you block essential cookies or localStorage, login, registration, checkout, and dashboard features may not work. To opt out of non-essential analytics, use your browser privacy controls, clear site data, or contact support for assistance. The cookie banner on public pages records your consent in localStorage so it is not shown on every visit.

10. Security

We use Firebase Authentication, Firestore security rules, role-based admin access, payment verification through trusted backend functions, audit logs, rate limits, and limited internal access controls to protect member data. No online service is completely risk-free, so members must keep passwords confidential, avoid sharing OTPs, and report suspicious activity immediately.

If we identify a personal data breach that affects your rights or privacy, we will take reasonable steps to contain the issue, investigate, notify affected users and authorities where required, and improve controls.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect legal changes, product changes, payment requirements, or improved controls. For material changes, we will aim to provide at least 30 days' notice through the website, email, WhatsApp, dashboard notice, or another reasonable method before the change takes effect, unless urgent legal or security reasons require faster action.
