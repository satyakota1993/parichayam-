Refund Policy

This policy explains exactly when Parichayam membership, matchmaker, verification, and add-on payments are refundable and how refund requests are reviewed.

Effective date: April 27, 2026 · Last updated: April 27, 2026
On this page Quick Summary 7-Day Cooling-Off After Matchmaker Assignment After First Suggestion Verification Fees Add-ons Gateway Fees How to Request Timelines Disputes

1. Quick Summary

Parichayam provides digital matrimony memberships and assisted matchmaker services. Once a service has started, profile work has begun, matchmaker capacity has been reserved, or match suggestions have been delivered, refund eligibility becomes limited.

Simple rule: A full refund is available only during the first 7 days after payment if no matchmaker has been assigned and no match suggestion has been delivered.

2. 7-Day Cooling-Off Period

You may request a full refund within 7 calendar days of payment if all of the following are true:

  • The refund request is received within 7 calendar days of successful payment.
  • No dedicated matchmaker has been assigned to your profile.
  • No match suggestion has been delivered to your account, WhatsApp, email, family console, or matchmaker workflow.
  • No paid add-on, verification, extra contact pack, profile boost, horoscope service, or family introduction support has been consumed or initiated.
  • The account is not under review for fraud, fake profile, abuse, chargeback, or violation of our Terms.

If these conditions are satisfied, Parichayam will approve a refund after verifying payment records and deducting applicable gateway charges as explained below.

3. No Automatic Full Refund After Matchmaker Assignment

For ASSISTED, PREMIUM, and ELITE plans, service begins when a matchmaker is assigned, reviews your profile, contacts your family, or reserves capacity for your case. After assignment, full refunds are not automatic because operational work has started.

If you cancel after matchmaker assignment but before the first suggestion, Parichayam may consider a pro-rated refund based on plan amount, elapsed time, work completed, matchmaker time, verification or support activity, and payment gateway charges. Pro-rated refunds are decided case by case and are not guaranteed.

4. No Refund After First Match Suggestion

Once the first match suggestion is delivered, the core paid service has commenced. Membership fees are not refundable after this point, even if you decline the suggestion, do not receive a response from the other family, pause your search, or later change your preferences.

Parichayam will continue to deliver the plan benefits available during the active validity period, subject to profile completeness, member responsiveness, safety checks, and Terms compliance.

5. Verification Fees

Aadhaar, income, or other verification fees are non-refundable once the verification process has been initiated with our internal reviewer or external verification partner. This is because partner checks, staff review, and audit logs may start immediately after the request.

If a verification payment is received but the verification process has not started due to a platform-side issue, we may refund the fee after review.

6. Add-ons and Extra Services

  • Extra contacts or connect packs are non-refundable once any contact from the pack is consumed.
  • Profile boosts are non-refundable once the boost is scheduled, activated, or displayed.
  • Horoscope matching or compatibility reports are non-refundable once generated or shared.
  • Unused add-ons may be refundable if requested within 7 days of purchase and before any part of the add-on is consumed.
  • Concierge introductions, family calls, or wedding-planning introductions are non-refundable once scheduling or coordination has begun.

7. Payment Gateway Fees and Deductions

Razorpay and other payment providers may charge gateway fees, bank fees, taxes, or payment processing charges. These are typically around 2% to 3%, depending on payment method and provider rules. Where a refund is approved, gateway fees and non-recoverable payment charges will be deducted from the refund amount unless Indian law or the payment provider requires otherwise.

Refunds are made to the original payment method wherever possible. We do not issue cash refunds for online payments.

8. How to Request a Refund

Email support@parichayam.co.in with the subject line "Refund Request". Include:

  • Member name and registered phone number.
  • Registered email address.
  • Razorpay payment ID or order ID.
  • Membership tier or add-on purchased.
  • Payment date and amount.
  • Reason for cancellation or refund request.

You may also submit the Contact form at /contact.html and choose the "Refund" category.

9. Review and Processing Timelines

  • Refund decision SLA: within 5 business days after receiving complete information.
  • Approved refund processing: 7 to 10 business days to the original payment method, subject to Razorpay, bank, UPI, card network, and holiday timelines.
  • If more information is needed, the review timeline starts after you provide the required details.

If the payment record cannot be verified, the account is under fraud review, or there is an active chargeback, refund processing may be paused until the issue is resolved.

10. Chargebacks

Please contact us first before raising a bank chargeback. Chargebacks are slow and can temporarily lock your payment record with the gateway. If a chargeback is raised while service remains active, Parichayam may suspend the account, pause matchmaker work, or restrict access until the payment dispute is resolved.
